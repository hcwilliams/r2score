Privacy Policy

R2 Score · Last updated October 5, 2026

The R2 Score app does not collect, store, or share any personal information. Everything you enter in the app stays on your device.

What the app stores

Match scores, match history, and your settings (such as haptic feedback) are saved only on your device, inside the app's own storage. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes this data.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No analytics, advertising, or tracking of any kind.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or location.
  • No servers: the app works fully offline.

Optional tips

The tip jar in Settings uses Apple's In-App Purchase. Apple processes the payment under Apple's Privacy Policy; we never see your payment details, Apple ID, or any personal information about you.

Links to other websites

The app links to the official RECF game manuals at games.recf.org. Those pages are run by the Robotics Education & Competition Foundation and are covered by their own privacy policy.

Children

R2 Score is built for student robotics teams. The app collects no information from anyone, including children under 13. Students who need help should ask a parent, teacher, or coach to contact support for them.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contacting support

If you contact us through the support form, it is hosted by Google Forms. We receive only what you type into it, including the email address you give us, and use it solely to reply to you. We don't share it or add you to any mailing list. Google's handling is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

Contact

Questions about privacy: use the support form and choose “Privacy question”.
